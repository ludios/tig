== Collection caveats
- A Triton nix build (-j8) was running during setup; all timing benchmarks
  started only after it finished (load < 2, 03:53 UTC onward).
- During collection the runner scripts' `pkill` daemon-kill pattern did not
  match the launcher's actual argv (bin/../src/daemon.ts), so ~32 stale
  daemons accumulated, idle, over the session (killed afterwards).  Data
  validity is unaffected: every runner also unlinks the bench socket, which
  detaches the old daemon and forces a genuinely fresh spawn — confirmed by
  observed cold behavior (e.g. sequence.csv first-visit giant = 15227 ms,
  vs 232 ms revisit; BM6 spawn log shows a distinct pid per sample).  The
  committed scripts carry the corrected pattern; the leaked daemons were
  idle (blocked in epoll) and consumed no measurable CPU during runs.
- BM7/BM8 "on"-mode runs go through the 15 s client frame timeout when a
  first visit exceeds it (tig receives the raw fallback); BM3 numbers show
  the daemon-side cost without that client timeout.
