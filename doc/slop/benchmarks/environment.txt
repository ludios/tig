== Collection notes
- All benchmarks ran sequentially on an otherwise idle machine.
- BM3's frame profiler was rewritten before this rerun to parse in O(n);
  the previous version's per-chunk Buffer.concat was quadratic and
  inflated times for multi-MB frames (affects only the superseded run).
- Daemon kill patterns in the runners now match the launcher argv
  (bin/../src/daemon.ts); the superseded run leaked idle daemons but
  socket unlinking kept its cold/fresh-daemon assumptions valid.
- BM7/BM8 'on'-mode first visits above 15 s hit the client frame timeout
  (tig gets the raw fallback); BM3 measures daemon-side cost without it.
- BM6's first rerun attempt aborted early (relative-path bug in run-bm6.sh,
  since fixed); it was rerun ~7 minutes after the main sequence under the
  same idle conditions.
